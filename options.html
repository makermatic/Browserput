Browserput

1. Device access

To list your outputs by name, the browser needs permission to see your audio devices. It asks for microphone access because that's how Chromium handles it. The extension opens the mic for a moment to get the permission, closes it straight away, and never records anything.

2. Your outputs

Play a short tone on each output to check which mixer channel it's on.

    4. Save file & backup

    Your saved sites live in the extension's storage inside your Brave profile. Extensions can't read or write files anywhere else on disk, so there isn't a file location to choose. Use these buttons to keep a copy or move your setup to another computer.

    5. Automatic routing (optional)

    Chromium only lets an extension capture a tab after you interact with it on that tab, so normally a saved site shows an orange ▶ badge. Click the icon once (or press the shortcut) and routing starts. The setting is remembered, so you never pick the device again. The click is needed once per tab after you open the browser.
